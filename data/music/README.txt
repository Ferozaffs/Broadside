Broadside's music. Drop tracks here, named by when they play:

  menu       the front page and the Shipwright
  sail       at sea, nobody close
  battle     guns going off or an enemy within ~400 m (until 14 s after the last)
  boss       a boss on the water
  victory    the round won (played once, then sail)
  defeat     the round lost, or an Odyssey run over (played once, then sail)
  port       ashore in the campaign (the port screen)

.ogg, .mp3, .wav or .qoa. More takes of the same one: battle_2.ogg, battle_3.ogg ... (up to 12);
they play in turn, crossfading one into the next. A missing one borrows another's:
boss -> battle -> sail, victory / defeat -> sail, port -> menu -> sail, menu <-> sail. No files: no music.

The music volume is on the front page and in the pause menu (saved in audio.cfg beside the game).
